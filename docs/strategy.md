# Strategy — UAE Web-Dev Client Acquisition

## Goal
Find small UAE businesses with no website via Instagram/Google Maps, pitch them with a
personalized demo, build sites with Claude Code, and charge for them.

## Approach
Builds on an earlier tested playbook: demo-first outreach, proven with wedding/event
photographers ("Lens & Co" demo, built in Google AI Studio, deployed on Vercel).

## Target categories (in priority order — best opportunity first)

1. Wedding vendors
2. Interior designers / contractors
3. Personal trainers / small gyms
4. Salons / barbershops
5. Boutiques / retail
6. Tutors / coaching
7. Real estate agents (requires Trakheesi compliance)
8. Clinics / dentists (requires DHA compliance)

## Workflow

1. Work one category at a time.
2. Gather 20-30 leads for that category.
3. Build one reusable demo template for the category.
4. Personalize per prospect — swap name, photos, color palette. Never send a raw copy-paste demo.
5. DM on Instagram.
6. Move to WhatsApp once they reply.
7. Close with a 50% deposit.

## Lead sourcing

- **Google Maps**: listings with no website field.
- **Instagram**: business accounts with no website link in bio.

## Pricing

- Offshore freelancers: AED 550-2,200.
- UAE budget shops: AED 500-2,500.
- **Recommended freelance pricing**: AED 1,500-2,500 (one-pager) / AED 2,500-4,500 (3-5 pages).
- Agencies: AED 8,000-30,000+.
- **First 3-5 clients**: price at AED 1,000-1,200 to build case studies, then raise to
  AED 1,500-2,500.
- Deposit norm: 50% upfront.

## Legal — unsolicited marketing (UAE, since Aug 2024)

UAE regulates unsolicited marketing (calls, SMS, social messages). Individual fines run
AED 5,000-50,000 for repeat violations. To stay compliant:

- Avoid bulk cold-messaging from a personal number.
- Keep Instagram DMs personalized and low-volume.
- Get a trade licence / freelance permit before operating at scale.

## What converts (from research)

- Demo-first, category-specific pitch angle.
- Moving to WhatsApp promptly after a reply.
- 50% deposit norm to lock in commitment.
