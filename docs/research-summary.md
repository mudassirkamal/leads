# Research Summary — UAE SME Web Presence

Findings from an earlier Advanced Research report, kept here for reference.

## Market opportunity

- An estimated 50-55% of UAE SMEs may lack a working website, extrapolated from 2020
  Central Bank data. Social media and WhatsApp function as the de facto storefront for many
  of these businesses.

## Pricing landscape

| Segment | Typical range (AED) |
|---|---|
| Offshore freelancers | 550 - 2,200 |
| UAE budget shops | 500 - 2,500 |
| Recommended freelance pricing (one-pager) | 1,500 - 2,500 |
| Recommended freelance pricing (3-5 pages) | 2,500 - 4,500 |
| Agencies | 8,000 - 30,000+ |

## Legal considerations

Since August 2024, the UAE regulates unsolicited marketing (calls, SMS, social messages).
Fines for individuals run AED 5,000-50,000 for repeat violations. Practical implications:

- Avoid bulk cold-messaging from a personal number.
- Keep Instagram DMs personalized and sent at low volume.
- Obtain a trade licence or freelance permit.

## What converts

- Demo-first outreach beats a cold pitch with no visual proof.
- A category-specific pitch angle (referencing the prospect's actual work) outperforms generic copy.
- Moving the conversation to WhatsApp quickly after a reply keeps momentum.
- A 50% deposit is the norm before starting build work.
