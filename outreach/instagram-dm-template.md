# Instagram DM Template — Demo-First Outreach

Paste-ready templates for the demo-first approach. Personalize every placeholder — never send
a template with placeholders still in it, and never bulk-send the same exact text to many
accounts back-to-back (see `docs/strategy.md` for the legal note on unsolicited marketing).

## First message (send after building a personalized demo)

```
Hi [Name]! I came across [Business Name] on Instagram — love [specific detail:
the styling in your last post / your recent [event type] work / your feed].

I noticed you don't have a website yet, so I put together a quick demo of what
one could look like for [Business Name]: [demo link]

No pressure at all — just wanted to share it since I thought it suited your
style. Let me know what you think!
```

## If they reply with interest

```
Glad you liked it! It's fully customizable — I can swap in your real photos,
colors, and copy. A site like this usually takes me [X] days to finish once
we lock the content.

Happy to hop on WhatsApp to go through details and pricing if that's easier —
what's a good number?
```

## Follow-up (send once, 3-4 days after no reply — never more than one follow-up)

```
Hey [Name], just floating this back up in case it got buried! Here's the demo
again: [demo link]

Totally fine if it's not the right time — just let me know either way :)
```

## Notes

- Placeholders to fill every time: `[Name]`, `[Business Name]`, `[specific detail]`, `[demo link]`, `[X]`.
- Keep the opening detail genuine and specific — generic compliments read as spam.
- Move to WhatsApp as soon as they show interest; close there.
- One follow-up max per lead. If no response, mark `Lost/Ghosted` in the tracker and move on.
